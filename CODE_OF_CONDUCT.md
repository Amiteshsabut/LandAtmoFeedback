# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free project.
Contributors should communicate constructively, welcome scientific scrutiny,
credit prior work, and distinguish evidence from interpretation.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal
attacks, deliberate misrepresentation of results, or publication of private
information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this
standard. Reports should be sent privately to the project maintainer through a
contact channel listed on the repository profile.
